Home Decor is an AI interior-design app. The user takes or selects a photo of a
room, picks a design style, and the app generates a redesigned version of that
room. A built-in "AI Designer" chat also answers decorating questions (styles,
colors, layouts).

No account or login is required — every feature is available immediately on
first launch.

An internet connection is required: the room redesign and the AI chat are
powered by a third-party generative-AI service, so results appear only when the
device is online. Please test on a normal connection.

How to test:
1) AI room redesign — On the Home tab tap "Try Now" (or "Try style" on any style
   card) → choose a photo with the camera or from the photo library → pick a
   style → tap generate. The redesigned image appears and is saved in the
   "My look" tab.
2) AI Designer chat — Tap the round AI button at the bottom-right → tap a
   suggested question or type your own → a written answer appears within a few
   seconds.

The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The Privacy Policy is
available in the app under Setting → Privacy Policy.
