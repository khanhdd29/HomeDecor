## Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [NGÀY PHÁT HÀNH]

Abodi ("the app", "we") is an AI interior-design app that turns a photo of your room into a redesigned concept and offers an AI design assistant. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: **there is no account, no advertising, and no tracking**, and the only data that leaves your device is the photo and text you choose to process with our AI service.

### 1. No account
Abodi has no sign-up and no login. The app uses a random, anonymous identifier stored on your device so the AI assistant can keep the context of a conversation. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any real-world identity, and you can reset it at any time by starting a new chat.

### 2. What we process, and why
- **Room photos.** When you choose or take a photo to redesign, that photo is sent to our AI image service to generate the redesigned result, which is returned to you. The photo is used **only** to create the design you asked for. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.
- **Chat messages (and an optional attached photo).** When you use the AI Designer, your message — and a photo you attach, if any — is sent to our AI service to produce a reply. It is used **only** to answer you.

We do **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, your location, your contacts, or device identifiers for tracking. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not ask to track you across other apps or websites.

### 3. What stays on your device
Your generated designs ("My look"), your history, your chosen language, and your settings are stored **only on your iPhone**. You can delete any design, or clear everything, at any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes this on-device data.

### 4. AI processing by our service provider
Redesigns and chat replies are produced by our AI image/processing service, which receives the photo or text described above solely to generate the result returned to you. [Xác nhận & nêu rõ chính sách lưu trữ của nhà cung cấp AI: ví dụ "Images are processed to generate your result and are not retained beyond what is needed to deliver it" — chỉ viết đúng với thực tế Code12.] We do not share your content with any other third party, and there are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

### 5. Children
Abodi is a general-audience app (rated 4+) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 6. Your choices
Because there is no account, you control your data directly in the app: choose which photos to process, delete saved designs, clear history, and reset the anonymous chat identifier. You may stop using the AI features at any time.

### 7. Security
Data sent to the AI service is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On-device data is protected by iOS app sandboxing.

### 8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
